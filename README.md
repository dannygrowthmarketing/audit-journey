# How I audit · from first conversation to revenue

Danny Deepak's audit method, run end to end on one real prospect: **Arise & Shine Unisex Salon**, Kalyan Nagar, Bengaluru (proposal stage, contract not signed).

**Live:** https://dannygrowthmarketing.github.io/audit-journey/

19 stages: the ask, market research, audience, web design, SEO & AEO, content, paid media, booking, the client journey, choosing the fixes, marketing vs pre-marketing, growth → retention → revenue, the 90-day plan, monthly data, data control, AI in the work, proof from past projects, 61 questions owners ask, and the reference documents.

- Built from public data checked 11–12 Oct 2026. Every claim is labelled Verified, Observation, Inference, Unknown, Illustrative or Reconstruction.
- Models (budget, revenue, marketing share) are illustrative and every input is adjustable. No client numbers are claimed.
- `docs/` holds the 27-page Growth Audit and the Marketing Psychology Playbook PDFs.
- `build/build.py` generates `index.html`; `build/qa.py` holds the 61 questions.
